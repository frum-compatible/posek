# Sample shailos

The phone page's five examples use original, hypothetical circumstances. The underlying issues appear in the sources below. These prompts are demonstrations, not additional benchmark results or reported incidents. The exact questions are in [the page script](../site/app.js).

| Example | Source basis | What makes the question substantive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Airbnb knife | [Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh De’ah 96:1](https://www.sefaria.org/Shulchan_Arukh%2C_Yoreh_De%27ah.96.1) and [96:2](https://www.sefaria.org/Shulchan_Arukh%2C_Yoreh_De%27ah.96.2); STAR-K, [Renting an Airbnb](https://www.star-k.org/articles/kashrus-kurrents/13344/renting-an-airbnb/) | A sharp raw food raises questions that visible cleanliness and two days without use do not settle. The knife's history, mixtures, and subsequent contact need separate attention. |
| Coffee after tasting the cholent | OU, [Milk & Meat: Soup Sampling](https://outorah.org/p/184516/); [Arukh HaShulchan, Yoreh De’ah 89:14](https://www.sefaria.org/Arukh_HaShulchan%2C_Yoreh_De%27ah.89.14) | Tasting, swallowing, and chewing are different facts. The scenario adapts the published liquid-sampling question to clear cholent gravy; it does not assume the same conclusion for every component of cholent. |
| Driving to the hospital on Shabbos | [Shulchan Aruch, Orach Chayim 330:1](https://www.sefaria.org/Shulchan_Arukh%2C_Orach_Chayim.330.1), [Mishnah Berurah 330:5](https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Berurah.330.5); Chabad, [Driving a Woman in Labor](https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/1638376/jewish/Driving-a-Woman-in-Labor-on-the-Sabbath.htm) | Childbirth, necessary care, and shinui are discussed together in the source. The Mishnah Berurah explicitly qualifies shinui by the absence of delay. The prompt requests immediate action first; an answer must not recommend unsafe driving or delayed medical help. |
| Paying the Shabbos babysitter | Chabad, [Hiring Babysitters](https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/6721225/jewish/What-You-Need-to-Know-About-Hiring-Babysitters.htm); Rav Daniel Mann, [Paying a Babysitter for Work on Shabbat](https://assets.torahtidbits.com/2022/11/30103935/Vayeitzei-1494-online-compressed.pdf) | A combined payment does not by itself settle how the employment is structured. The weekday task, cancellation terms, and whether the arrangement is genuinely combined require analysis. |
| The airline “mezonos” roll | STAR-K, [Pas Habah B’Kisnin](https://www.star-k.org/articles/kashrus-kurrents/1188/pas-habah-bkisnin/) | The recipe and using the roll for a meal are distinct inputs. The sample adds a mid-meal discovery: the answer must examine the blessing already made as well as what to do next. |

Source material was inspected on 2 October 2026. These links provide the basis for the questions; they do not replace checking the sources and applicable practice when answering them. No modern source translations are bundled here.
