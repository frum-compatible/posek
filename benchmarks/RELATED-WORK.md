# Related evaluation work

Halachic AI evaluation already has precedents. These projects use different tasks and conditions; their scores cannot be compared directly with Posek's pilot.

- Shlomo Friedman's [LLM Semicha, Part I](https://llmod.substack.com/p/llm-semicha-part-i), published February 16, 2025, reports an exploratory comparison using a Rabbanut examination question and discusses wrong attributions and citations. This is the author's experiment, not an official examination result or a population ranking of rabbonim.
- Tsuriel Vizel and Avraham Guez's [Beit Yosef argument-extraction benchmark](https://github.com/Avraham2048/NLP_Benchs_for_LLM) describes 31 debates from 18 source files, researcher-created annotations, and LLM grading. Its task is to recover the structure and attribution of a machlokes from text. That is narrower than deciding a practical shailah.

These precedents suggest useful checks: preserve who says what, distinguish a quoted opinion from an adopted ruling, and verify each decisive locator. Posek has not run either external dataset. The repositories and articles are linked for inspection; their datasets and scoring claims have not been independently validated here.
