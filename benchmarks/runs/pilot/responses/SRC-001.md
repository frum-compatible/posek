The initial blessing is **borei peri hagafen**. Cooking alone does not change it. The Mechaber explicitly includes wine “בין חי בין מבושל”—“whether uncooked or cooked” (my translation)—under this blessing. Your stated facts fit that rule. [Shulchan Arukh, Orach Chayim 202:1](https://www.sefaria.org/Shulchan_Arukh,_Orach_Chayim.202.1)

An edition caution: the English translation retrieved from Sefaria says “raw or diluted,” but the Hebrew word here is **מבושל**, “cooked.” The ruling above follows the inspected Hebrew.
