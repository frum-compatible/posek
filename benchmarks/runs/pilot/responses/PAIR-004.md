Following the Rema’s practice, recite **two blessings**, even without an interruption:

1. **Lehaniach tefillin** on the arm tefillin.
2. **Al mitzvat tefillin** on the head tefillin.

The Rema expressly records this practice in his gloss. The Mechaber’s different ruling is one blessing for both; your stated family practice determines which instruction applies here. [Shulchan Arukh and Rema, Orach Chayim 25:5](https://www.sefaria.org/Shulchan_Arukh,_Orach_Chayim.25.5)

The Rema also recommends saying **Barukh shem kevod malkhuto le’olam va’ed** after the second blessing. Say it **after securing the head tefillin properly**, so it does not interrupt between that blessing and putting them on. This is an additional declaration, not a third tefillin blessing. [Mishnah Berurah 25:21](https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Berurah.25.21)
