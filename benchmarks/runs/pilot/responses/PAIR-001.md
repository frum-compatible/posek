**No. *Borei peri ha’etz* does not fulfill the initial blessing over this cucumber.** The Mechaber expressly rules that the tree-fruit blessing over ground produce does not discharge the obligation. Recite the correct blessing, **borei peri ha’adamah**, before eating. [Shulchan Arukh, Orach Chayim 206:1](https://www.sefaria.org/Shulchan_Arukh,_Orach_Chayim.206.1)

The reverse mistake has a different result: *ha’adamah* over tree fruit is valid after the fact. The same se’if explicitly distinguishes the two directions.
