**Yes—you have fulfilled the initial blessing. Do not replace it with another blessing.** The Mechaber rules explicitly that someone who said *borei peri ha’adamah* over tree fruit has fulfilled the obligation after the fact. That applies to the apple as you have defined it. [Shulchan Arukh, Orach Chayim 206:1](https://www.sefaria.org/Shulchan_Arukh,_Orach_Chayim.206.1)

Before making the blessing in the ordinary case, choose *borei peri ha’etz*. The valid result after a mistake does not change the proper initial choice. [Orach Chayim 202:1](https://www.sefaria.org/Shulchan_Arukh,_Orach_Chayim.202.1)
