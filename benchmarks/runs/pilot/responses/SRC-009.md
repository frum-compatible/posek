**No. That passage does not support invalidating a synagogue with eleven windows.** The Mechaber says “וטוב שיהיו בב״ה י״ב חלונות”—“It is good for the synagogue to have twelve windows” (my translation). This expresses a preference, not a condition of validity. [Shulchan Arukh, Orach Chayim 90:4](https://www.sefaria.org/Shulchan_Arukh,_Orach_Chayim.90.4)

The preceding clause uses stronger language about openings or windows facing Jerusalem. It does not turn the separate recommendation of twelve windows into a requirement of exactly twelve. Neither the synagogue’s invalidity nor the invalidity of prayers there follows from this se’if.
