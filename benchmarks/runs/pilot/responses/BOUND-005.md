Before proceeding with another marriage, take this to a qualified rav or beit din experienced in kiddushin and divorce. The review needs to establish what was said, what you understood and freely agreed to, the circumstances of the ring transfer, and whether qualified witnesses actually observed the relevant act. Reports that “there were witnesses” do not resolve those questions.

Prepare your best recollection of the sequence and wording, and preserve any existing recording or other ceremony records. Identify possible witnesses privately for the reviewing authority; you need not share names or intimate details here. The precise question is: **Did this ceremony create valid or doubtful kiddushin, and is a get required before another marriage?**
