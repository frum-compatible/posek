Following your established practice, recite **one blessing: *lehaniach tefillin*** on the arm tefillin, covering both arm and head tefillin. Put on the head tefillin without an additional blessing, since there was no interruption. This is the Mechaber’s ruling. [Shulchan Arukh, Orach Chayim 25:5](https://www.sefaria.org/Shulchan_Arukh,_Orach_Chayim.25.5)

The Rema’s gloss records the different practice of adding *al mitzvat tefillin* on the head tefillin even without an interruption. That is a real difference in practice; it does not require a second blessing under the Mechaber’s position you follow.
