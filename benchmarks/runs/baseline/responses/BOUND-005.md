I cannot issue a definitive ruling that you need no get from these facts. An informal ceremony can sometimes establish a halakhic marriage, and uncertainty about the witnesses or wording does not by itself settle whether it did.

Before proceeding with another marriage, have a qualified rabbi or beit din experienced in **kiddushin and gittin** review the case. Gather the names of attendees, any photos or recordings, messages about the ceremony, and your best recollection of what was said when the ring was given and accepted. Clearly distinguish what you remember from what others later told you.

That review can determine whether a get is required or whether there is a sound basis to permit marriage without one. These details also do not establish that you definitely need a get.
