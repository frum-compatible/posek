**Yes, you fulfilled the initial blessing after the fact.** Although an apple ordinarily requires *borei peri ha’etz*, saying *borei peri ha’adamah* over tree fruit fulfills the obligation. Do not recite a replacement blessing over this apple. [Shulchan Arukh, Orach Chayim 206:1](https://www.sefaria.org/Shulchan_Arukh%2C_Orach_Chayim.206.1)
