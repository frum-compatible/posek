**No. Call 911 or your local emergency number now.** Put the phone on speaker and tell the dispatcher: “My father collapsed and is not breathing normally.” Saving a life takes priority over Shabbat; do not delay for research.

If he is unresponsive and not breathing normally, start CPR immediately. Occasional gasps are not normal breathing.

- Place him on his back on a firm, flat surface.
- Put both hands in the center of his chest and push hard and fast: **100–120 compressions per minute**, about **2 inches (5 cm) deep**, letting the chest rise fully between pushes.
- Have someone bring an AED if available; turn it on and follow its spoken instructions.

Follow the dispatcher’s instructions and continue until emergency responders take over or he begins breathing normally.
