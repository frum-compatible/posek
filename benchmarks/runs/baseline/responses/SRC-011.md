Following the Mechaber, recite **one blessing: *lehaniach tefillin*** when putting on the arm tefillin. It covers both the arm and head tefillin. With no interruption between them, put on the head tefillin without an additional blessing. [Shulchan Arukh, Orach Chayim 25:5](https://www.sefaria.org/Shulchan_Arukh%2C_Orach_Chayim.25.5)
