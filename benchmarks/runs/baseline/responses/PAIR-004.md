Following the Rema’s practice, recite **two blessings**, even without an interruption:

1. **Lehaniach tefillin** when putting on the arm tefillin.
2. **Al mitzvat tefillin** when putting on the head tefillin.

After the second blessing and putting on the head tefillin, say **Baruch shem kevod malchuto le’olam va’ed**, as the Rema recommends. [Rema, Orach Chayim 25:5](https://www.sefaria.org/Shulchan_Arukh%2C_Orach_Chayim.25.5)
