The initial blessing is **borei peri hagafen**. Cooking alone does not change it: Shulchan Arukh explicitly includes cooked wine in this ruling. Given that your drink remains grape wine, its blessing remains hagafen. [Orach Chayim 202:1](https://www.sefaria.org/Shulchan_Arukh%2C_Orach_Chayim.202.1)
