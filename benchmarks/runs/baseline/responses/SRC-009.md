**No.** O.C. 90:4 says “וטוב שיהיו בב״ה י״ב חלונות”—it is good for a synagogue to have twelve windows. That expresses a preference; it does not make exactly twelve windows a condition of validity or invalidate a synagogue with eleven. The passage separately discusses openings facing Jerusalem.
