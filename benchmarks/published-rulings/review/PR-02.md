The answer also preserves the consequential distinctions after Shabbos. Actual Jewish reacquisition leads to immersion, with the usual blessing for the stipulated definitely obligated metal utensil; [YD 120:11](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/שולחן_ערוך_יורה_דעה_קכ) and [Rav Melamed’s explicit gift-back recommendation](https://ph.yhb.org.il/17-31-07/) support that branch.
