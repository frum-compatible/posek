- **b:** Rema expressly excludes separate external blood from the usual expulsion protections. Rema’s customary sixty assessment for blood is confirmed explicitly by Shakh 76:5; no conflict should be invented from 105:9’s exceptions for other completely nonfatty prohibitions. [Shulchan Arukh/Rema YD 105:9; Shakh YD 76:5](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/שולחן_ערוך_יורה_דעה_קה_ט)

- **b–c:** The special runoff rule applies to normally expelled blood with juice and is not established for separate blood deposited externally. The cited distinction reconciles the stacked-pieces and external-droplet cases. [Tosafot Chullin 113a, s.v. אין מחזיקין; Beit Yosef YD 76 quoting Rosh](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/טור_יורה_דעה_עו)
