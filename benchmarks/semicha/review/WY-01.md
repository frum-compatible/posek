## R08

SHA-256: `63ac5a13e38b093bcf2409c9b40c9858cac6ee3c810c1d45c3147f6a44755af8`

12/12; development pass. Correctly rejects a blanket river-only purchasing rule, provides a substantial initial-use basis for ordinarily irrigated kosher aravos, and keeps validity separate from the disputed preference. It verifies the irrigation issue through Arukh HaShulchan and identifies application to modern cultivation as an application. No requested subpart is missing; no critical failure found.

Required omissions: none identified.

Source findings:

- **basic validity:** The sources define a species usually growing by streams and validate that species even in mountains or desert. River location cannot validate an otherwise invalid species. This supports rejecting the seller’s universal location requirement. [Shulchan Arukh OC 647:1 and Rambam, Shofar/Sukkah/Lulav 7:3–4](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/שולחן_ערוך_אורח_חיים_תרמז)
- **validity versus preference:** The Mishnah validates shel baal; the baraita includes baal and mountains. Rashi defines baal by adequate rainfall and separately states a river-growth preference. Both responses correctly avoid equating baal with artificial irrigation. [Mishnah/Gemara and Rashi, Sukkah 33b](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/סוכה_לג_ב)
- **strict view:** Tosafot’s two-derivations objection and instruction to use river-grown branches are accurately reported. Precision note: Arukh HaShulchan 647:5 explicitly understands this strand to affect validity even after the fact. Both responses label it stronger/stricter than Rashi’s preference; neither claims Tosafot agrees with the permissive conclusion. [Tosafot, Sukkah 34a, s.v. ורבנן למקדש](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/סוכה_לד_א)
- **permissive view and custom:** Rosh records ordinary practice and his teachers’ lack of insistence, answers Tosafot through the species definition, and invokes Rambam. Tur expressly reports the initial/after-the-fact dispute and the Rosh’s initial permission. Both answers faithfully preserve it. [Rosh Sukkah 3:13; Tur OC 647](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/רבינו_אשר_על_הש%22ס/סוכה/פרק_ג#סימן_יג)
- **preferred practice:** Taz gives a positive reason to use non-river growth to demonstrate the law. Mishnah Berurah reports the preference and Taz’s lack of insistence. The candidates do not erase the preference or turn it into a universal rule. [Taz OC 647:2; Mishnah Berurah 647:3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/ט%22ז_על_אורח_חיים_תרמז)
- **artificial irrigation:** The discussion explicitly recognizes human-watered land and says no source establishes its exclusion. The valley/level-ground explanation is the author’s proposed reconciliation; the answers correctly avoid presenting it as a new prerequisite to the accepted species-based permission. [Arukh HaShulchan OC 647:5–7](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/ערוך_השולחן_אורח_חיים_תרמז)
- **purchasing application:** Species identification and branch condition are relevant purchase checks. The advice is consistent with the codified defects and the preference for sound foliage. These checks do not depend on river provenance. [Shulchan Arukh OC 647:1–2; Mishnah Berurah 647:7–12](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/משנה_ברורה_על_אורח_חיים_תרמז)
