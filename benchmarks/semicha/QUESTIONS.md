# The questions and answers

These are the four condensed English prompts used in the source-assisted development pilot. Three come from a Chief Rabbinate examination paper; the fourth adapts a publicly announced WebYeshiva course question. They are not complete examinations. [Method and results](RESULTS.md).

## CR-01

(a) Compare Rishonim on cooking poultry with milk and benefiting, citing Gemara. (b) Explain Agra’s poultry/cheese teaching in Tosafot and Rambam, their conclusions, and Beit Yosef YD89. (c) May a Jew cook meat for a non-Jew in a dairy pot that is not ben-yomo?

[Original source](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/exams_rabanut_iyar_2026/he/100.05.26%20-%20%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A8%20%D7%95%D7%94%D7%99%D7%AA%D7%A8.pdf) · [Posek answer](runs/posek/responses/CR-01.md) · [No-skill answer](runs/baseline/responses/CR-01.md) · [Source review](review/CR-01.md)

## CR-02

(a) Compare three Rishonim positions on ingested milk recovered from a slaughtered animal’s stomach, and Shulchan Arukh/Rema. (b) Shulchan Arukh forbids setting cheese with such milk salted in stomach skin or soaked there for a day: explain the difficulty and resolution. (c) Explain Shach’s challenge, two resolutions, and Rema’s rationale for the sixtyfold condition when clear stomach milk sets cheese.

[Original source](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/exams_rabanut_iyar_2026/he/100.05.26%20-%20%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A8%20%D7%95%D7%94%D7%99%D7%AA%D7%A8.pdf) · [Posek answer](runs/posek/responses/CR-02.md) · [No-skill answer](runs/baseline/responses/CR-02.md) · [Source review](review/CR-02.md)

## CR-03

(a) Give three Rishonim explanations permitting stacked pieces during salting, with practical differences. (b–c) Analyze external cold blood dripping onto meat during its salting period or roasting, explaining Shulchan Arukh/Rema and reasons.

## WY-01

An aravos seller claims that only willows grown along a riverbank should be bought for Sukkos, and that artificially irrigated willows are acceptable only after the fact. Evaluate that claim and explain whether it should determine which aravos a person purchases. Identify the relevant sources and distinguish validity from preferred practice.
