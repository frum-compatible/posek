# Audience and shiur settings

Choose sources and depth for the requested shiur, using the hashkafah, audience, and format the user supplies. These are editable teaching presets for Posek. They make no claim about a group's ability and do not rule on who may learn a particular sefer.

## Resolve the brief

Use the user's explicit instructions first: sources to include or leave out, depth, language, time, and audience. Then apply the selected teaching preset and use the hashkafah profile for any remaining defaults. A user can change any of these without changing their communal identity.

| Audience preset | Default preparation |
| --- | --- |
| General | Follow the requested topic and length. Explain a technical term when needed. |
| Ladies' Parsha Shiur | Use the source plan below for the selected hashkafah. Develop a clear reading of the parsha and a supported point in avodah. |
| Women's Beis Medrash | Use Gemara and mefarshim where the topic calls for them, under any hashkafah. Work through the source argument; adjust depth to the request. |
| Yeshiva Seder Iyun | Develop the kushya, sevara, and relevant nafka minah. Keep a proposed chiddush distinct from a cited shittah. |
| Baalei Batim Shiur | Explain the practical or textual question first. Give enough of the sugya to support the conclusion, within the allotted time. |
| Teenagers | Use an accessible explanation and a concrete example suited to the occasion. Keep the Torah content and source attribution intact. |

If the request says only "a women's shiur," do not infer a curriculum or level of knowledge. Use the supplied audience details, or ask whether the user wants a parsha shiur, a Gemara-based shiur, or another format when that choice affects the piece.

## Ladies' Parsha Shiur defaults

These defaults apply only when the user chooses this particular preset and has not specified a source mix.

| Selected hashkafah | Sources for the delivered shiur |
| --- | --- |
| Lakewood Yeshivish, Modern Yeshivish, Out-of-Town Yeshivish, Torah im Derech Eretz, or Chassidish | Pesukim, mefarshim, and suitable mussar or hashkafah sources. Leave Gemara analysis out of the delivered piece unless requested. |
| Modern Orthodox, Open Orthodox, or Dati Leumi | Pesukim and mefarshim, with Gemara included where it develops the argument. Explain the relevant sugya rather than adding a daf as decoration. |
| No profile or a custom profile | Follow the requested sources. If the distinction matters and no preference is given, ask one short question about the desired source mix. |

Default to a developed explanation in accessible language for this parsha format. That is a choice about the event, not the audience's intellectual capacity. A request for iyun overrides it. Selecting Women's Beis Medrash also calls for a full source-based Gemara treatment under any profile when the topic requires it.

## Source mix and depth

Accept `source mix: preset`, `Tanach and mefarshim`, `Gemara and mefarshim`, or a custom list. Accept `depth: preset`, `accessible`, `developed`, or `iyun`. The user can give these instructions in any host app.

Accessible means explain necessary terms and keep one main argument. Developed means give the important textual steps and address the strongest relevant objection. Iyun means work through the definitions, disputed readings, and consequences the question requires. None of them calls for padding, name-dropping, or an invented kushya.

If the delivered shiur leaves out Gemara analysis, still check the sources behind the commentary you teach. When Rashi cites a Gemara, read enough to understand his comment. You can teach Rashi without presenting the sugya, but do not misattribute Chazal's explanation to yourself or fabricate an alternative source. If the requested topic cannot be treated responsibly within the chosen source mix, explain the conflict and offer a suitable adjustment.

Source mix limits the presentation of a vort or shiur. It never authorizes omitting a controlling source from practical psak. Questions about whether a particular form of study is permitted require their own halachic analysis; the audience selector does not decide them.

## State the chosen approach when useful

For a substantial shiur, a short preparation note may help: "Ladies' Parsha Shiur; Modern Yeshivish; Tanach and mefarshim; developed; five minutes." Keep it outside the spoken text. For a short answer, simply follow the brief.

```text
Use Posek. Audience: Ladies' Parsha Shiur.
Hashkafah: Modern Yeshivish. Source mix: Tanach and mefarshim.
Depth: developed. Four minutes on the parsha, with mareh mekomos afterward.
```

```text
Use Posek. Audience: Women's Beis Medrash.
Hashkafah: Open Orthodox. Source mix: Gemara and mefarshim.
Depth: iyun. Compare the two readings I supply and explain the nafka minah.
```

```text
Use the Lakewood register for a Women's Beis Medrash shiur.
Include the Gemara and Tosafos. Assume the audience knows the sugya.
```
